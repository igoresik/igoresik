<h2 align="center">About me</h2>

I'm Georgy, a junior software developer from near Torres Vedras, Portugal.

I recently finished a three-year professional course in Systems Management and Programming. My final project was an **Android app for managing classes**, with a payments page, a scheduling calendar and lesson summaries. Teachers can create their own organisation inside it. It scored **20/20** in the final presentation.

During two work placements at a music and multimedia shop I repaired and tuned computers, set up dual-boot Windows and Linux machines, improved the local network, redesigned the web store and helped customers at the counter. Right now I'm an Erasmus+ intern at **The Discovery Partnership** in Cork, working on websites, databases and accounting support.

I'm looking for a junior role in **software development, IT/networking or security**, starting January 2027, in the Lisbon area or remote.
